# BANZAI UI - Credits

## BANZAI UI
BANZAI UI is created and maintained by **Darren (HARDKORH)**. The bamboo construction, the green → emerald → violet → purple identity gradient, the BANZAI branding and the **original class identity artwork for all thirteen classes** are Darren's own work.

Licence: see the LICENCE and notices shipped in the package.

## Origin
BANZAI UI grew out of a private restoration of the JiberishUI Shinto/Bushido look and is published with the permission Darren established with its original author. The original author does not endorse, support or maintain BANZAI UI; please do not contact JiberishUI about it.

## Separate projects
BANZAI UI runs on top of, and does not include, the following. Each is a separate project by its own authors under its own licence:
- **ElvUI** (required).
- **JiberishMedia** (required; separate download, its own licence).
- Optional: **Details!**, **ElvUI_mMediaTag** + **!mMT_MediaPack**, **ElvUI_EltreumUI** (Eltruism), **JiberishIcons**, **!KalielsTracker**, **MinimapButtonButton**.
