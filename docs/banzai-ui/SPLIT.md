# BANZAI SPLIT frames

**BANZAI SPLIT** is the second frame style. Player sits at the lower centre, Target at the upper centre; each has its health bar to the **left** of a central class / cast circle and its resource bar to the **right**, the name under the health bar and the spec under the resource bar. While you cast, the circle shows the spell icon and a bamboo cast bar above the frame shows progress, name and time.

Setting: **UNIT FRAMES > FRAME STYLE > Frame style** > *BANZAI SPLIT*. Per character; a reload applies it. Switching back to BANZAI CLASSIC restores the classic frames exactly.

## BANZAI SPLIT section

- **Player position X / Y**, **Target position X / Y** - the centres of the circles, in units from the screen centre and from the bottom / top edge. They move at once. **Reset positions** returns the defaults.
- **Frame scale** - bar length and height. **Central icon scale** - the class / cast circles. **Bar thickness** - the Player / Target health and resource bars. A reload applies these three.
- **HP text** - current health over the outer end of the health bar, the percentage beside the circle (Focus: inside its bar).
- **Resource text** - the percentage beside the circle, the current value over the outer end (NPCs without a resource show no resource side).
- **Enhanced target nameplate** - your current target's nameplate gets a small class / cast circle and a green -> purple outline; every other nameplate is unchanged. BANZAI SPLIT only.
- **Party style** - party and arena allies in the split construction (class circle in the middle of the bar, name across its lower part). Off: the classic party frames. A reload applies it.
- **Preview BANZAI SPLIT** - labelled sample frames (*SAMPLE DATA*) wherever the frames in use are; hidden in combat.

## SPLIT CAST BAR section

- **Bamboo cast bar** - the bar above the split Player / Target. A cast fills left to right, a channel drains right to left, in the unit's class colours; steel when it cannot be interrupted; *INTERRUPTED* for a moment in crimson. Turning it on or off moves the class bar (combo points, holy power ...) - a reload applies that part.
- **Spell name** - centred on the bar. **Timer** - remaining time at the right end. Both at once.

Focus in SPLIT shows a ring that fills with the spell name above it.

## IDENTITY ART section

**Identity art** chooses what the central and party circles show: *BANZAI* (Darren's original class identities; default), *BANZAI everywhere*, *FABLED*, *CLASS COLOUR*. The enhanced target nameplate and the BANZAI COMPACT Details rows keep their small art. Per character; Character Sync carries the choice, never the art files.

## Not changed

Spells, bars, macros, keybinds and talents. The PvP frames (arena enemies, trinkets, CC / dispel states) are governed by the PVP page and stay hands-off secure frames.
