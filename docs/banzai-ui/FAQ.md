# FAQ

**What is BANZAI UI?**
A complete ElvUI-based look: bamboo panels, a green -> emerald -> violet -> purple identity gradient, two frame styles (BANZAI CLASSIC and BANZAI SPLIT), three panel materials, Darren's original class identity artwork, a Banzai objective tracker, cast history, AFK screen, and Character Sync to carry one setup to your other characters.

**What do I need?**
ElvUI and JiberishMedia (both installed separately; JiberishMedia has its own licence and is not in the zip). Details!, mMediaTag, Eltruism, JiberishIcons, Kaliel's Tracker and MinimapButtonButton are optional and used when present.

**Nothing happened after I logged in.**
That is intended. Nothing applies automatically. Open ElvUI options > BANZAI (or `/banzai`) and press **Apply / repair Banzai** on HOME.

**Will it overwrite my ElvUI profile?**
BANZAI writes its own ElvUI profile and records the profile you were using first. **BACKUP & RESTORE > Restore pre-Banzai settings** switches back.

**Does it touch my spells, bars, macros or keybinds?**
No. Not on apply, not on sync, not on restore. Only visual settings are written.

**Which is the default frame style?**
BANZAI CLASSIC. BANZAI SPLIT is a per-character choice under UNIT FRAMES > FRAME STYLE.

**What is the default font?**
BANZAI Default = Shojumaru at 105 %. Automatic uses it for fully supported strings and the Information Font otherwise. Seven display faces and the Information Font are selectable. [FONTS.md](FONTS.md)

**Why is a heading suddenly in the small font?**
The chosen display face cannot show one of its characters (Xangda Shiny, for example, has letters only), so the whole string is drawn in the Information Font. That is the rule, not a bug.

**Can my characters have different looks?**
Yes. Frame style, identity art and the split positions are per character. Character Sync only moves what you ask it to, on the character you press the button on.

**Does Character Sync copy my whole saved-variables file?**
No. It carries the list under CHARACTER SYNC > WHAT SYNCS and nothing else; gameplay data is never copied.

**I use Kaliel's Tracker.**
With the Banzai tracker on, Kaliel's is hidden through its own visibility setting and restored when you turn the Banzai tracker off. Its files are not touched.

**Is BanzaiSland required?**
No. BanzaiSland is a separate addon. With both installed the integrated BANZAI SLAND launcher opens either addon's settings and BANZAI UI's small wordmark steps aside.

**Does it work on WoW Forever?**
This release is for Retail (Interface 120105) only.

**Is this JiberishUI?**
BANZAI UI grew out of a private restoration of the JiberishUI Shinto/Bushido look and is published with the permission Darren established with its original author. The original author does not endorse, support or maintain BANZAI UI; please do not contact JiberishUI about it.
