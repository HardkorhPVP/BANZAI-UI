# Cast history

Your own last casts appear between Player and Target, just above the action bars, newest on top, older entries fading. Only your own cast events and the game's spell names and icons are used; nothing is read about other players.

Page: **BANZAI > LAYOUT > CAST HISTORY / BOTTOM PANEL / BUFFS > CAST HISTORY**.

## Settings

- **Show cast history** - on / off.
- **Entries** - 1-6 casts kept on screen.
- **Opacity** - 30-100 %.
- **Scale** - 60-150 %.

All four apply at once.

## Behaviour

- Spell names use the small text font; icons are the game's own.
- In BANZAI SPLIT the Player frame's default position is chosen to stay clear of the cast history and the action bars.
- The cast history's appearance is carried by Character Sync; it is never a record of anything - it shows only what you cast in this session.

## Not changed

Your cast bar (ElvUI's), spells, keybinds; no combat-log data is stored.
