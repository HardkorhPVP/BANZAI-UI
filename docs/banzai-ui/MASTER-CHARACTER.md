# The Banzai Master character

The **Banzai Master** is the one character whose BANZAI setup your other characters follow through [Character Sync](CHARACTER-SYNC.md). There is one master per account at a time.

Page: **BANZAI > CHARACTER SYNC > STATUS > BANZAI MASTER**.

## Set the master

1. Log in on the character that looks the way you want.
2. If it is not on BANZAI yet, apply BANZAI first (HOME, or CHARACTER SYNC > **APPLY BANZAI**).
3. Press **SET AS BANZAI MASTER**. The confirmation says what is captured: its BANZAI-owned visual configuration, nothing of its gameplay.

## Capture

- The master's setup is captured **by itself whenever the master logs out**. Change a setting on the master, log out, and the capture is current.
- **CAPTURE MASTER NOW** captures immediately without logging out (only shown on the master).
- **VERSION** tells you which BANZAI version captured the setup. After upgrading BANZAI UI, log in on the master once so the capture includes any new settings; until then other characters use the defaults for settings the old capture did not know.

## Change the master

Press **SET AS BANZAI MASTER** on a different character. The previous master simply becomes a synced character; nothing on it changes.

## Stop having a master

**RESTORE PRE-BANZAI** on the master puts it back to its pre-BANZAI setup and it stops being the master. Characters already synced keep their current setup; set a master again whenever you want.

## What the master never carries

Spells, action bars, macros, keybinds, talents, equipment, Details history, chat history, currencies, quests and unrelated addon settings. The tracked cooldowns of the cooldown bars are class abilities and stay with each character. Class colours and class / spec art always come from the character being synced.
