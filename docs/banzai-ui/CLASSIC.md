# BANZAI CLASSIC frames

**BANZAI CLASSIC** is the default frame style: the approved Banzai unit frames with bamboo ends, the large class / spec circles, the identity line (*NAME - SPEC*) and health drawn inside the bars.

Setting: **UNIT FRAMES > FRAME STYLE > Frame style** > *BANZAI CLASSIC*. Per character; switching needs a reload. Coming back from BANZAI SPLIT returns the classic frames exactly as they were.

## What you see

- Player and Target mirrored left and right, bamboo bundles at their outer ends, the large circular class portrait (Jiberish Fabled art through mMediaTag) as the anchor.
- Health value in the display font and percentage in the small font inside the bar; over the empty part the text turns ghosted.
- Focus, Target-of-Target, Pet, Party, Arena and Boss frames in the same language; small identity circles with the Fabled spec icon on Focus, Party, Arena and player Boss frames.
- Nameplates with the Banzai bar texture and a glow on your current target.

## Settings that shape the classic frames

Tab **UNIT FRAMES > PLAYER / TARGET / FOCUS / TOT**:

- **WIDTH > Player / Target / Focus / ToT width** - a share of Bushido's original width (BANZAI 1.0 uses 50 %); **BANZAI 1.0 width (50 %)** resets it. A reload applies it.
- **PLAYER & TARGET > Height** and **Distance from the screen edges** move Player, Target, Focus and Target-of-Target together; **Reset positions** puts them back.
- **PLAYER & TARGET > Target cooldown bars** - Bushido's cooldown display between Player and Target (ElvUI bars 2 and 4). Off in BANZAI; the spells stay on their bars. A reload applies it.
- **FOCUS, TARGET-OF-TARGET, PET > Target-of-Target size**, **Reset Target-of-Target**, **Identity circles**.

Tab **ROLE LAYOUTS**: DPS and HEALER placements for party, raid, arena allies, arena enemies and boss frames, each with a preview toggle. **LAYOUT > DPS / HEALER PLACEMENT** has **Reset DPS positions** and **Reset HEALER positions**.

Tab **UNIT FRAME PREVIEW**: labelled sample frames (*SAMPLE DATA*) for every unit, **Frame style** to preview Classic or Split without changing the frames in use, **SHOW ALL** / **HIDE ALL**. Hidden in combat.

## Identity art

**UNIT FRAMES > FRAME STYLE > IDENTITY ART > Identity art**: *BANZAI (classic circles: Fabled) - default* keeps the Fabled art in the classic circles; *BANZAI everywhere* puts Darren's class artwork in the Focus / Party / Boss circles too; *FABLED*; *CLASS COLOUR*. Per character; Character Sync carries the choice only.

## Not changed

Which spells are on your bars, keybinds, macros, talents, and ElvUI settings outside the Banzai profile.
