# AFK screen

When you go AFK out of combat, BANZAI shows a dark green glass screen: your character turning slowly in the middle (or your class art), **BANZAI**, your name, class / spec line, level and zone, with the Banzai bamboo tree and the green / purple light behind. The HUD is hidden while it shows and comes back exactly as it was.

Page: **BANZAI > AFK SCREEN**.

## AFK SCREEN section

- **Enable** - Banzai's AFK screen. ElvUI's own AFK screen is switched off while this is on.
- **Character model** - your character in the middle of the screen; off shows your Fabled class art there instead.
- **Model rotation** - the model turns slowly through a full circle. **Seconds per turn** - 8-60.
- **Background opacity** - 40-95 %.
- **Branding intensity** - 30-100 %: BANZAI, the bamboo tree and the green / purple light.

## SHOWN section

Toggle each line: **Character name**, **Class / spec**, **Level**, **Zone**.

## PREVIEW section

**PREVIEW AFK SCREEN** shows the screen now so you can tune it without waiting to go AFK.

## Behaviour

- Any key or click, moving, entering combat, a queue pop, a ready check or a loading screen ends the screen at once.
- The screen never shows in combat.
- All AFK settings are carried by Character Sync.

## Not changed

Your AFK status (the game's), ElvUI's AFK settings beyond its screen being off while Banzai's is on.
