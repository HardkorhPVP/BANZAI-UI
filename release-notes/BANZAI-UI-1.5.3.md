# BANZAI UI 1.5.3

## Chat (Retail / Midnight)

- The chat text now fills the inside of the bamboo CHAT frame; the frame itself is unchanged.
- The chat tabs (General, Loot / Trade, your own) stand on the frame's top border beside the CHAT title, in the title's font at half its size, with a dark outline.
- The selected tab is fully opaque, the others half opaque and brighter under the pointer.
- The CHAT frame's top border sits slightly lower, in line with the tabs.
- The tabs are the game's own: click, right-click menu, drag, scrolling and the edit box work as before.

## Details (Retail / Midnight)

- The DAMAGE and HEALING meters fill the inside of their frame: wider, slightly taller, ten rows instead of nine.
- The frame, the titles and the centre divider are unchanged; Details' own menus, tooltips and segment controls work as before.

## Compatibility

- Retail / Midnight 12.1.5.
- WoW Forever 1.60.1: the same package installs; the chat and Details layout there is unchanged from 1.5.2.
